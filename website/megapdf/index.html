Open. Fix. Save. Done.

MegaPDF does the one job most people actually have with a PDF: check the boxes, drop in your signature, and save it back — without an account, a subscription, or your document ever leaving your device.

Android and iPhone / iPad apps are in testing now — Google Play and App Store releases are on the way.

✓ Check any box

Real form fields and plain printed squares alike — MegaPDF recognizes drawn checkboxes that other apps treat as decoration.

✎ Sign like you mean it

Draw your signature or photograph the one on paper — the white background disappears. Place it, move it, resize it until it sits right on the line.

💾 Save without fear

Every save is verified before it touches your original, so a failed write can never corrupt the file someone sent you.

🔍 Find any word

Search the whole document as you type. Every match lights up, the counter tells you how many, and one tap walks you to the next.

🔒 Private by design

Zero permissions, zero network access, zero analytics. There is no server for your documents to go to — and the code is open, so anyone can check.
