Done in three steps

No upload, no account, no learning curve. Open the PDF the way you open anything else, and it is ready to fill in.

Open it

From the email it came in, from Files, from a share sheet, or with a double-click on your computer. MegaPDF opens it right where it is.

Fill it in and sign

Tick any box, even plain printed squares. Type on any line, in a size and face that match the form. Then add your signature: draw it, type it, or use a photo of the one on paper.

Send it back

Save, then Share it on your phone or attach it from your computer. Too big for email? Shrink for email makes a smaller copy and tells you honestly when it can't.

Everything else you'll need, one day

Most days it's fill in and sign. For the other days, it's all there, on every platform.

✏Fix the document's own text

Wrong date or a misspelled name? Click the line and retype it. MegaPDF keeps the document's own font where it can, and Undo puts the original back exactly.

■Redact for real

What you mark comes out of the file, so no other app can copy it or search for it. Whiteout is there too, and says plainly that it only covers.

✉Shrink for email

Make a smaller copy that fits as an attachment. Your original stays as it was, and if the copy wouldn't be smaller, MegaPDF tells you instead.

🔍Find any word

Search the whole document as you type, including scanned pages, which are read on your device (everywhere but Linux).

⊞Page tools

Rotate a page scanned sideways, delete one, reorder by dragging, insert pages from another PDF, or save the pages you picked as their own file.

◐Reading mode

One tap hides the toolbars and leaves the page, in Normal, Sepia for a long read, or Night for a dark room.

⧉Tabs on your computer

On Windows, Mac and Linux, each PDF opens in a tab of the same window, with its own undo, find and zoom.

🔑Protected PDFs

Open one with its password, respect its owner's restrictions, or set, change and remove a password yourself.

XLVery large files

A 2.5 GB PDF opens straight away, without using gigabytes of memory. Banner-size pages measure at their real size.

💾Saves you can trust

Every save is checked before it replaces your original, so a failed write can never ruin the file someone sent you.

M↓Text out, as Markdown

Export the document's text with its headings, lists and filled-in values. On a computer, megapdf-cli does the same from a terminal.

🌐Your language New in 2.4

English, French and Spanish, for Latin America and for Spain, following your device's setting.

Questions people ask

Is it really free?

Yes. No ads, no subscription, no premium tier and nothing to buy inside the app. MegaPDF is open source under the Apache 2.0 licence, made by Electric RV.

Is my signature a digital signature?

No. MegaPDF places an electronic signature: the one you draw, type or photograph, put on the page where it belongs. A digital signature is a different thing, backed by a certificate, and MegaPDF doesn't make one. If a document already carries one, MegaPDF tells you. If you're unsure which kind a form needs, ask whoever sent it.

Does it need the internet?

No. It works the same in airplane mode, because it never connects anywhere. Your documents and signatures stay on your device.

Will the person I send it to be able to open it?

Yes. MegaPDF saves an ordinary PDF that opens in any PDF reader. Your ticks, text and signature are saved into the file itself, so they see exactly what you did.

What languages does it speak?

English, French and Spanish (for Latin America and for Spain). It follows your device's language setting.

Something isn't working. Who do I ask?

The support page answers what people ask most and helps you write to us about anything it doesn't. One person reads those messages, and nothing you type there is sent anywhere until you send it yourself.
