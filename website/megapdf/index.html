New in 2.0

■ Redact, and it really is gone

Mark what has to come out — a name, an address, a picture — and MegaPDF takes it out of the file rather than covering it over. What was underneath is gone: no other app can copy it or search for it. On the desktop, whiteout (Cover on the Mac) still only covers, and now says so.

✏ Fix the document's own text, far more often

MegaPDF used to refuse a correction when it could not promise the rest of the page would stay put. It now makes those changes. Over a 22,000-edit test run, every one of the 112 edits it used to refuse goes through, and none of them disturbs the page.

⧉ Very large PDFs

A 2.5 GB file used to take a minute to open and gigabytes of memory. It now opens straight away. Banner-size pages measure at their real size, and pages full of pictures draw several times faster.

🔑 Protected PDFs

Open one with its password. If its owner restricted what may be changed, MegaPDF respects that and says so, and the owner password unlocks it. You can also set a password on a document, change it, or remove it.

▤ One row of tools

The toolbar is a single row, with the pickers appearing beside the tool they belong to and everything else under More.

🇫🇷 MegaPDF speaks French

Canadian and European French throughout the app. It follows your device's language.

✓ Check any box

Real form fields and plain printed squares alike — MegaPDF recognizes drawn checkboxes that other apps treat as decoration.

T Type on any line

Tap or click where the answer goes and type it. Pick the size and the face so it matches the form you are filling in — then drag it into place, or open it again to fix a typo.

✎ Sign like you mean it

Draw your signature, type it, or use a photo of the one on paper — the white background disappears. Place it, move it, resize it until it sits right on the line.

💾 Save without fear

Every save is verified before it touches your original, so a failed write can never corrupt the file someone sent you.

🔍 Find any word

Search the whole document as you type. Every match lights up, the counter tells you how many, and one tap or Enter walks you to the next.

🔒 Private by design

No permissions to grant, no network access, no analytics. There is no server for your documents to go to — and the code is open, so anyone can check.
