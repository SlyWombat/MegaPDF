Getting Help

Have this ready before you call:

l Your phone number, address and company name.

l Machine model and serial number.

l The version of the software you are running.

l A description of what the machine was doing.

l Whether the fault repeats.

Not Markers

l A lone line like this one is prose, not a list.

Three lines beginning with a real word follow.

A bird flew past the window. A second one followed it south. A third stayed on the wire.
